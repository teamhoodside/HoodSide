Privacy Policy for HoodSide

HoodSide is committed to bench-level data privacy. Our core operational philosophy is simple: your experimental protocols, formulas, hotfixes, and audit logs remain strictly under your control.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No Protocol Data: HoodSide processes Excel workbooks and QR optical transfers entirely in local memory (RAM) or sandboxed device storage. Your assay designs, master mixes, and reagent details are never transmitted to our servers.
  • No Cloud Syncing of Experiments: Unless you explicitly share an exported Excel run summary through your device's native OS share sheet, your bench data does not leave your hardware.

2. In-App Purchases & Licensing

HoodSide offers a lifetime Pro unlock processed securely through Apple App Store (In-App Purchases) or Google Play Billing, mediated by our purchase verification partner, RevenueCat.

  • RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase tokens and device identifiers to validate active entitlements.
  • No lab data, protocol names, or personal identities are linked to payment transactions.

3. Camera Usage

The app requests camera permissions exclusively for the bench-side optical QR transfer utility. Video feeds are parsed in real time to reconstruct protocols and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

4. Corporate / Compliance Mode

In Corporate Mode, protocol state runs strictly in ephemeral device memory, and completed run exports are sanitized of raw formula definitions according to institutional research guidelines.

5. Contact

For questions or verification requests, contact the development team at GitHub: teamhoodside/HoodSide.
